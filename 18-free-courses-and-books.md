# Stage 18: Free Courses and Books as a Spine

The repositories in this course are for building, but a few free courses and books give the whole map a spine. Items marked "search-confirmed" were found on 25 Nov 2026, and the rest are from memory.

## Core free textbooks and courses

### Modern Robotics, Lynch and Park
The textbook has a free preprint, free YouTube lectures filmed on a lightboard, a free simulator for exercises and code in several languages. It is also a six-course Coursera specialisation, equivalent to a year-long university course, with courses on kinematics, dynamics, motion planning and control, and a mobile manipulation capstone (search-confirmed). If you only pick one spine, pick this one. Its home page is [modernrobotics.org](https://hades.mech.northwestern.edu/index.php/Modern_Robotics).

### Underactuated Robotics, Russ Tedrake
Free online notes and lecture videos from MIT, with Drake notebooks, covering nonlinear control, trajectory optimisation, walking and manipulation (search-confirmed). It sits well after Modern Robotics for control and locomotion.

### Stanford CS223A, Introduction to Robotics
Stanford Engineering Everywhere published the full course with lecture videos, slides, assignments and exams for free (search-confirmed).

### Other university lists
A course list in the search mentions MIT 6.832 Underactuated Robotics, UC Berkeley CS287 Advanced Robotics, Stanford CS235 on robot design and CS205A on mathematical methods for robotics, vision and graphics (search-confirmed). Class Central also keeps a large list of free robotics courses that you can filter by level.

## How to use a course alongside the repositories

Read or watch one chapter, then reproduce its main example in code from the matching repository before moving on. When a chapter feels abstract, build the smallest hardware or simulation demo of it. Keep a notebook of equations you can derive without looking.
